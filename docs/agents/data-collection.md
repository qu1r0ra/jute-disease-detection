# Data collection for feasibility

Status: the owner closed acquisition on 2026-10-31 after J23's final retrieval attempt; J10 is dropped after repeated download failures. Add a source only with a new owner decision. Cohort and rights decisions live in issue #105.

This describes the current source-gathering approach, not an approved dataset, experiment, or manuscript claim. The owner directed a fresh, broad collection on 2026-10-31, including the sources cited in the repository and informal public releases. Keep the historical collection and test-exposure evidence as reference.

1. Discover and record downloadable dataset releases, including possible mirrors and derivatives; individual web and social-media photos are outside the active search. Cover jute disease, general leaf, pest and thermal sources; retain other crops bundled in relevant releases. Document search terms, dates, coverage and access failures; describe the search limits rather than claiming internet-wide completeness.
2. Obtain accessible releases with potentially relevant labels for inspection before deciding what to retain. A supplied folder name, annotation or source description is a lead to check, not a verified diagnosis. Record standalone unlabeled sources for possible later use; retain unlabeled files bundled with relevant releases. Preserve each release separately, including its original archive or files and source/version information. Record download date, size and checksum, or the access obstacle. Keep raw acquisitions separate from the historical pool.
3. After attempting every identified downloadable, potentially labeled release and another targeted search for gaps, record successes and access barriers, then inspect every obtained release. Keep inaccessible sources on a follow-up list; add and inspect later arrivals before finalizing the dataset.
4. Inspect each obtained release for contents, labels, provenance, terms and relationships to other releases. Track uncertain overlaps and known or possible historical test exposure. A source listing or image count does not establish independent captures or valid diagnoses.
5. Bring unresolved inclusion, label, grouping and evaluation choices to the owner before constructing or freezing a cohort. Resolve image-use rights before publication or redistribution, in line with research context.

Use the ignored `docs/research/jute-source-register.md` when present for source-specific leads, and `docs/research/2026-10-31-collection-log.md` for this acquisition's receipts and decisions. Those private notes are not prerequisites in a fresh checkout; preserve equivalent evidence where they are absent.
