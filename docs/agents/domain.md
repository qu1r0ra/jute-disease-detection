# Decision records

Read relevant records in `docs/adr/` before architecture or methodology changes. Surface conflicts before superseding a decision.

Record confirmed decisions when reversal would be costly or the rationale is surprising and real alternatives were considered. Include context, decision, and reason; add alternatives, consequences, and evidence when helpful. Mark superseded records with their successor and preserve history. Keep one `docs/adr/` layout and add warranted records only; do not create placeholders, a glossary, or a domain-modeling workflow.
