# Historical configurations

These Lightning CLI and grid configurations reproduce the original six-class course study reported in the repository README. They are a superseded record: keep them unchanged for reproduction, and do not use them as defaults for new runs.

The revised related-image study uses a different design (MobileNetV2 on three pretraining routes, policy-specific splits, settings tuned on a reserved subset). Its settings come from the planning map, [issue 98](https://github.com/qu1r0ra/jute-disease-detection/issues/98), and its decision issues, and new configurations will live outside this directory.
